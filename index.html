Before you start

Fair questions

Start your growth today

7 days free, then $7/month at launch. Cancel anytime.

Will this get me clients?
Nobody can promise what other people do, and I won’t. The community teaches the system in order, so you always know the next step and do the work yourself.
Is it a lot of work?
It’s self paced. The Quick Start gives you one task a day for 7 days, about 30 minutes each. Every day opens as soon as you join, and each one says what to do, why it comes first, and when you’re done.
I get most of my clients from referrals. Is this for me?
Yes, if you want more than referrals. Referrals are great. They’re just hard to plan around. The system gives you steps you run yourself, starting with your offer and your outreach.
I’ve tried courses and tactics before. What’s different?
You work one step at a time, in order. The Quick Start gives you one task a day. Once a month you can also watch me run the same system live on my own client work.
What does personal feedback mean at $7?
I reply to your posts in the community. Written, in public, with no set turnaround. Deeper, private help is available inside the community.
What does it cost?
7 days free, then $7/month at launch, or $42/year. Cancel anytime. The price goes up as members join, and you keep the rate you joined at while you stay subscribed.
