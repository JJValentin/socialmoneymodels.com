Why it stalls here

Quick Start · Day 1

The offer in one line

Write one sentence that says who your service helps, the outcome, and the price.

Why this first: People need to understand what you do before they can decide whether it matters to them.

The steps

  1. Write the name of the service you currently sell.
  2. Name one specific kind of person who buys it.
  3. Write the outcome that person wants in their own words.
  4. Add your current price or the basis for a quote.
  5. Cut any words from the sentence that you would not say aloud.

Fill in

I help specific person clear outcome through your service for price or clear quote basis.

Use the actual price you charge. If the job varies, state what determines the price.

Done when: One completed offer sentence is saved in your working document.

Quick Start · Day 4

The Step 0 reactivation message

Offer something useful, personally, to people who already know your work.

Why this first: A useful message can reopen a conversation that went quiet.

The steps

  1. Pick up to five past customers, old leads or warm contacts.
  2. Pick one short, useful thing you already have, like a checklist, a guide or a post you wrote. Nothing yet? Build it first with Day 3 of the Quick Start, then send.
  3. Replace the gaps with details from the real relationship.
  4. Send each message individually through the existing channel.
  5. Record the send date beside each name.

Copy and send

Hi name, I was thinking about our work on real context. I put together a short format to help buyer small task. Thought it might be useful for you. Would you like me to send it?

The opening refers to something true. No booking link or pitch attached.

Done when: Your first group has received the personalized messages.

Quick Start · Day 6

Track replies and answer the first responses

Record what happened and give each reply a useful first response.

Why this first: A request can disappear in a busy inbox if you do not record it.

The steps

  1. List every open conversation in one sheet.
  2. Record each reply against the correct person.
  3. Answer each one using the reply under Copy and send.
  4. Record the next action for each open conversation.

Copy and send

Thanks for explaining. It sounds like brief, accurate reflection. Which part is hardest to work out right now?

Ask one question at a time. Let the answer guide your next reply.

Done when: One dated tracker update records all replies and responses so far, or records zero replies.

Why it stalls here

Why it stalls here

The call is doing work the offer should have done. If what they told you and what you offered don’t match, or the price arrives as a surprise, “let me think about it” is the polite way out.

What moves it

What moves it

Go in knowing their problem in their words, with an offer that answers it and a real price. Then help the person decide whether your service fits.

Your first move

Review your last three calls

Free, and not a Quick Start lesson: the Quick Start doesn’t teach calls. In the trial, start with Day 1, the offer in one line, since the call is often doing the offer’s work.

Do this today

Your last three calls

Write down what each person said, in their words, next to what you offered.

Why this first: Their words show what they care about and what needs explaining.

The steps

  1. Pick your last three sales calls.
  2. For each, write the problem they described, in their words.
  3. Write the offer and the price you gave them.
  4. Mark where the two don’t match.
  5. Save one question you want help with next.

Fill in, once per call

They said: their problem, in their words.

I offered: your offer and price.

The gap: what didn’t match.

Keep the record factual.

Done when: All three calls are written down, with one question for next time.
