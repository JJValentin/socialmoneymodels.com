Who runs this site

Social Money Models is run by Joshua Valentin. Questions about this page go to joshua@valentinmarketing.com.

What this site collects

This site asks for your email in two places: at the end of the free quiz, and on the EchoOS “Get notified” form. When you submit it, it goes to a private Google Sheet that only I can see. Submitting it does not sign you up for my newsletter.

The sheet holds your email, where you signed up, the date, and the visit note described below. For the quiz, it also holds your answers and the step they point to, so I know what you’re working on. If you sign up from EchoOS, it notes that you asked to hear when EchoOS opens. I don’t sell or share this list.

Analytics, ads and cookies

This site uses Google Tag Manager to load two measurement tools: Google Analytics (GA4), which shows me how many people visit and which pages and links they use, and the Meta Pixel, which shows me which Facebook and Instagram ads bring visitors and signups. Both set cookies in your browser and send Google and Meta information such as the pages you view, your browser and device, your IP address, and actions like finishing the quiz, asking to hear about EchoOS, or clicking through to Skool or Substack. Their policies apply to that information: Google and Meta.

To opt out, install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and choose how Meta uses this information for ads in your Meta ad preferences. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Where visitors come from

When you visit, this site saves a small note in your browser’s local storage about how you arrived: the first page you landed on, the date, and, when present, the campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source) and the name of the website that sent you, not its full address. If you come back later from another website or a tagged link, it also notes that most recent source. Each part is kept for up to 90 days, only in this browser on this device, and your browser may clear it sooner.

The note stays in your browser unless you submit your email on the quiz or the EchoOS form. Then it is saved with your signup in the private Google Sheet. To remove it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, for security and reliability, and tells the signup form which country a visit comes from; that country is saved with your signup.

Other services

Social/Money, my free newsletter, runs on Substack, and the community runs on Skool. If you join either one, their policies apply: Substack, Skool, Cloudflare (hosting) and Google (the signup sheet).

Your choices

If you subscribe to Social/Money on Substack, you can unsubscribe anytime with the link at the bottom of every email. To ask what we have about you or to have it deleted, email joshua@valentinmarketing.com.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
