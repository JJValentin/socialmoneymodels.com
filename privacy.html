Who runs this site

Social Money Models is run by Joshua Valentin. Questions about this page go to joshua@valentinmarketing.com.

What this site collects

This site asks for your email in two places: at the end of the free quiz, and on the EchoOS “Get notified” form. When you submit it, it goes to a private Google Sheet that only I can see. Submitting it does not sign you up for my newsletter.

The sheet holds your email, where you signed up, and the date. For the quiz, it also holds your answers and the step they point to, so I know what you’re working on. If you sign up from EchoOS, it notes that you asked to hear when EchoOS opens. I don’t sell or share this list.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad tracking.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, for security and reliability, and tells the signup form which country a visit comes from; that country is saved with your signup.

Other services

Social/Money, my free newsletter, runs on Substack, and the community runs on Skool. If you join either one, their policies apply: Substack, Skool, Cloudflare (hosting) and Google (the signup sheet).

Your choices

If you subscribe to Social/Money on Substack, you can unsubscribe anytime with the link at the bottom of every email. To ask what we have about you or to have it deleted, email joshua@valentinmarketing.com.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
